# One open Repeating bill at a time

A Repeating bill only creates its next Bill when the current one is marked paid. It never creates new Bills on a schedule by itself. We chose this because many of our Persons have memory loss or anxiety: if a Person misses a month, a pile of Overdue copies of the same bill would be frightening and confusing, while one clear Overdue item tells the truth calmly. The trade-off is that a long-unpaid Repeating bill does not show how many periods were missed, which we accept.
